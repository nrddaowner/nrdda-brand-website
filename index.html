    How do I report a bug in NEET AI, Detective IRA, or HisaabKiKitaab?+

    File a ticket with the form below, or email nrdda.owner@gmail.com directly. Include the product name and what you did right before it broke.

    Is my data secure in HisaabKiKitaab?+

    Your ledger is stored on your device first, so the app works with no signal. While you're signed in it also syncs to a private cloud backup under your own account, so a lost phone doesn't mean a lost year of records. Nobody else can read it, it's never sold, and the app has no access to your bank, SMS, or card details — every entry is one you typed. Full detail in the HisaabKiKitaab privacy policy.

    Which products run in a browser?+

    NEET AI runs on the web and on Android. HisaabKiKitaab, Detective IRA, and IRA Ball Sort are Android only.
