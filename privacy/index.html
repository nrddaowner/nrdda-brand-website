What every NRDDA app has in common

  • We never sell your personal information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone, in any app.
  • We ask for the minimum permissions each app genuinely needs, and each policy explains every one of them line by line.
  • No app reads your contacts, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone, or location.
  • Everything is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • You can always get your data deleted — through the app where an account exists, or by clearing app data where one does not.
  • One person answers the privacy inbox. Requests are handled by Dipak Chouhan directly, within 30 days and free of charge.

Questions about any of these policies go to nrdda.owner@gmail.com.
