01. Overview

Irapot, formerly HisaabKiKitaab (“the App”, “we”, “our”), is a personal money diary and a way to split shared costs, for Android, published by NRDDA Tech, Hyderabad, India. This policy applies only to this App.

Key takeaways

  • Sign in with your email. We email you a one-time code; there is no password. We never ask for your phone number, contacts or location.
  • Every entry is one you typed. The App never connects to your bank, never reads SMS, and never sees card, UPI or net-banking details.
  • Your diary is encrypted on your phone and kept on our own server (GameHub, run by NRDDA Tech), so it comes back on a new phone. It is never shown to anyone else.
  • Split is shared only with your group. The spends, settle-ups, receipt photos and comments you add to a group are seen by its members and no one else.
  • Reading bills and screenshots happens on your phone. The text recognition runs on the device; the picture is not sent to us or to Google for reading.
  • The App is ad-supported. Ads come from Google AdMob. Irapot Plus, a one-time purchase through Google Play, removes them and adds extras.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers, lenders, credit bureaus or data brokers. The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

02. What stays on your phone

Your entries, budgets, goals, bills, Split groups you are in and settings are kept in a database inside the App’s private storage, encrypted (SQLCipher). Receipt photos on the phone are encrypted with a key held in Android’s Keystore. This copy is what lets the App work with no signal; it is brought in line with our server when you are online.

Reminders (the daily diary reminder and bill reminders) are scheduled and worded on your phone.

03. What our server receives

Your account, your diary and Split run on a server operated by NRDDA Tech (GameHub). All traffic to it uses HTTPS. We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Required

Your account

Data received: your email address (confirmed with a one-time code sent from no-reply@nrdda.com), the name you choose, and for each phone you use: a random install ID, the App and Android version, the device model, and your IP address (to keep the service secure and prevent misuse). Signing in on a phone signs you out of your other phone.

Automatic

Your money diary

What you enter is kept on our server under your account, so it is there on a new phone: entries (amount, type, category, date, and any note you type), accounts and their balances, budgets, goals, bills and repeating entries, tags, no-spend days, challenges and your settings. With Irapot Plus, earlier versions of a changed or deleted entry are kept for 30 days so you can bring them back.

Not received: anything from your bank, SMS, contacts or other apps.

Irapot Plus

Receipt photos

With Plus, receipt photos you attach are backed up to our server, up to your photo space. A photo on a Split spend is visible to that group’s members. Clearing a photo removes it everywhere.

Automatic

Notifications

While you are online, the App checks about every 15 minutes for changes in your Split groups, and for replies, likes and new comments, so it can tell you. It asks only for your own groups. If you allow push messages, the server keeps your phone’s Firebase Cloud Messaging token to send them.

04. Split groups

Shared with your group

Groups, spends and settle-ups

A group’s name and emoji, its members’ names, the spends and settle-ups added to it, who changed what, and its receipt photos are kept on our server and shown to that group’s members only. People join through your invite link, QR code or code. If you invite someone by email, we keep that email so they can join when they sign in with it; we do not send them an email, and it is deleted after 30 days.

Shared with your group

Comments and reactions

Comments, replies, likes and emoji reactions you add to a group’s spend are kept on our server with your name and the time, and shown to that group’s members. Reports and blocks you make are never shown to others. You can delete your own comments at any time; when a spend or group is deleted, its comments go with it. Commenting, liking and reacting come with Irapot Plus.

05. What Google’s services collect

  • Google AdMob (free plan only): may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, device model and Android version, and ad interactions, for showing ads, limiting how often you see them and preventing fraud. Where the law requires it, Google’s consent form is shown before any ad. Your diary is never sent to AdMob or used to choose ads. Rewarded videos that pay coins are confirmed by Google directly with our server.
  • Google Play Billing: processes Irapot Plus, coin packs and Day Passes. We receive the product, purchase token, order ID and status, never your card, UPI or bank details.
  • ML Kit text recognition and document scanner (Google Play services): read bills and payment screenshots you choose, on your phone. The pictures and their text are not sent to Google or to us for reading; only the entry you save is kept.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push messages, if they are switched on.

06. Coins, Day Pass and Irapot Plus

Free accounts spend coins on new entries; the server keeps your coin balance and its history. A Day Pass makes entries free for a time. Irapot Plus is a one-time purchase through Google Play: no ads, no coins, receipt photo backup, version history, bigger groups and Split comments. Our server records the purchase (token, order ID, status) to give you what you bought and to bring it back on a new phone.

09. Children

Irapot is made for adults managing their money and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 18 under India’s DPDP Act). If you believe a child has used the App, email us and we will delete their data.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: where consent applies, clear the App’s data to see Google’s consent form again.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Notifications: turn each kind off in the App, or all of them in Android’s notification settings.
  • Your comments and photos: delete any of yours at any time.
  • Access or deletion requests: email us. We will send you what our server holds about you and delete it on request, within 30 days and free of charge. This applies under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and California’s CCPA/CPRA, and we extend it to everyone wherever they live.

11. Deleting your data

  • Delete my Irapot data (Me tab, confirmed with an emailed code): wipes your diary from our server and all your phones, with its history. Your account, coins and Plus stay.
  • Close account (Me tab, confirmed with an emailed code): you are signed out at once, and after 14 days your account, diary, receipt photos and group memberships are deleted from our server; sign in within those 14 days to cancel. Your comments’ words are deleted; replies others wrote stay, under “Deleted comment”. A group you started stays for its other members. Purchase records are kept without your name, as Google Play and tax rules need them.
  • On the phone: uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes the phone’s copy.

12. Security and retention

  • In transit: all traffic to our server and to Google services uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • On the phone: the database and receipt photos are encrypted.
  • Our server: your data is kept while you use the App, and deleted as in section 11. Cleared receipt photos are removed for good after 7 days.
  • Ads and purchases: kept by Google under its own retention policies.

No system is completely secure, but we use the protections above and collect as little as we can.
