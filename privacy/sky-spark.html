01. Overview

Sky Spark: Fireworks Puzzle (“the App”, “we”, “our”) is a casual puzzle game for Android, published by NRDDA Tech, Hyderabad, India. This policy applies only to this App.

Key takeaways

  • No account, ever. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos or location.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Levels, boosters and settings are stored in the App’s private storage.
  • We run no server for this game and the App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or Firebase SDK. Anything that leaves your device goes to a Google service, never to a server of ours.
  • Reminders are worked out on your phone. When they arrive and what they say come from your progress on the device. There is no push server, and you can switch them off.
  • Backups are a file you keep. Save backup writes a small file wherever you choose; we never receive it.
  • The game is ad-supported. Ads come from Google AdMob. A one-time purchase, No Ads, removes the banner and the ads between levels.
  • We never sell your personal information, to anyone.

02. What stays on your device

On device only

Game progress and settings

Stored: the next level to play, how many boosters you hold, how many times you have missed each level (so the game can offer a free hint), which pieces you have already looked up, and your music, sound and vibration settings.

Also on device: simple counters the App uses to pace its ads, such as how many levels have passed since the last ad and how many ads were shown today, and whether you own No Ads. These never leave your phone.

Removing it: Settings › Start over in the game resets your progress; clearing the App’s data or uninstalling deletes all of it. We have no copy.

On device only

Reminders

The game can remind you to play: once the day after you last played, and at most three more times (after 2, 7 and 14 days) if you stay away, then never again. Android asks for your permission first, and nothing is posted between 10 pm and 8 am.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. To time them and fill in their words (your next level, your world and how much of it is lit), the App keeps the time you last opened it and the hour you usually play. There is no push-notification server and no notification token is sent anywhere.

Turn them off with Settings › Remind in the game, or in Android’s notification settings.

Your file

Backup file

Settings › Save backup writes a small file with your next level, booster counts, misses per level and how many pieces you have met. Android’s own file picker lets you choose where it goes, such as your phone’s storage or a cloud drive you already use; if you pick a cloud drive, that provider’s terms apply to the file.

Restore backup reads a file you pick and asks before replacing your progress. The App opens only files you choose, and we never receive a copy. No Ads is not in the file; Restore purchases brings it back through Google Play.

On device only

Home-screen widgets

If you add a Sky Spark widget, it shows your next level or your current world, drawn on your phone from the progress above. It refreshes about twice a day and sends nothing anywhere.

03. What Google’s services collect

The App uses the following Google services. Each receives data directly from your device under Google’s own privacy policy, acting as a service provider to us.

Advertising

Google AdMob

The App shows a banner ad on the Home screen (never on the puzzle board). From level 20 it may show an interstitial ad after you clear a level: at most one every few levels, never right after another, and a limited number per day. You can choose to watch a rewarded video ad to get a booster (an extra match, an undo or a peek); these are always your choice.

Data collected by AdMob: the Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, language, mobile network, coarse location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and anti-fraud signals.

Purpose: showing ads, measuring them, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choice (see section 05) and your device’s ad settings.

Updates

Google Play In-App Updates

The App asks Google Play, on your phone, whether a newer version of the game is out. If one is, it may show a panel on Home (at most every few days) and one notification per new version, and the Update button hands over to Google Play. You can always choose Later.

Data processed: the check is handled by the Google Play Store app already on your phone, under Google’s privacy policy. It does not send your game progress anywhere.

04. Optional purchase

The game is fully playable without buying anything.

Optional

Purchases through Google Play

The App sells one thing: No Ads, a one-time purchase that removes the banner and the interstitial ads. Rewarded videos stay available if you want boosters from them.

Data processed: product ID, purchase token, order ID and purchase status, used to give you what you bought and to restore it after a reinstall or on a new phone (Settings › Restore purchases).

Payment details: payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card number, bank details or billing address.

05. Ad consent

In places where the law requires your permission before ads can use your data, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. If you decline personalised ads, AdMob shows only non-personalised ads.

Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy options in the game.

Everywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some phones). Deleting it stops personalised ads in every app on your device.

08. Children

Sky Spark is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU). If you believe a child has used the App and you want their data removed, email us and we will help, including by contacting Google on your behalf.

09. Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: open the game and go to Settings › Privacy options (shown where consent applies).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Remove ads: buy No Ads from the Shop.
  • Reminders: turn them off with Settings › Remind in the game, or in Android’s notification settings.
  • Backup files: they are yours; delete them wherever you saved them.
  • Local game data: Settings › Start over in the game, or Android Settings › Apps › Sky Spark › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the App.
  • Access or deletion requests: email us. We hold no personal data about you ourselves, so we will tell you what the App stores on your phone and work with Google on anything held by AdMob or Google Play. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. This applies under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and California’s CCPA/CPRA, and we extend it to everyone wherever they live.

10. Security and retention

  • In transit: all traffic to Google services uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • On device: game data is kept in the App’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox.
  • Ads and purchases: kept by Google (AdMob and Google Play) under its own retention policies.
  • Local data: kept until you clear it or uninstall the App.
  • Backup files: kept where you saved them until you delete them; uninstalling the App does not remove them.

No system is completely secure, but we use the protections above and collect as little as we can.
