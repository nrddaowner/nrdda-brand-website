01. Overview

Sky Spark: Fireworks Puzzle (“the App”, “we”, “our”) is a casual puzzle game for Android, published by NRDDA Tech, Hyderabad, India. This policy applies only to this App.

Key takeaways

  • No sign-up needed. The game works without an account. Saving with your email is optional, and we never ask for your name, phone number, photos or location.
  • Your progress lives on your phone. If you own Sky Spark Plus and save with email, a copy is also kept on our server so it can come back on a new phone.
  • A small server of ours (GameHub, run by NRDDA Tech) checks purchases and keeps the optional account and cloud save. The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or Firebase SDK.
  • Reminders are worked out on your phone. When they arrive and what they say come from your progress on the device. There is no push server, and you can switch them off.
  • Backups are a file you keep. Save backup writes a small file wherever you choose; we never receive it.
  • The game is ad-supported. Ads come from Google AdMob. A one-time purchase, Sky Spark Plus, removes the banner and the ads between levels and adds cloud save.
  • We never sell your personal information, to anyone.

02. What stays on your device

On device only

Game progress and settings

Stored: the next level to play, how many boosters you hold, how many times you have missed each level (so the game can offer a free hint), which pieces you have already looked up, and your music, sound and vibration settings.

Also on device: simple counters the App uses to pace its ads, such as how many levels have passed since the last ad and how many ads were shown today, and whether you own Sky Spark Plus. These never leave your phone.

Removing it: Settings › Start over in the game resets your progress (and your cloud save, if you have one); clearing the App’s data or uninstalling deletes what is on your phone.

On device only

Reminders

The game can remind you to play: once the day after you last played, and at most three more times (after 2, 7 and 14 days) if you stay away, then never again. Android asks for your permission first, and nothing is posted between 10 pm and 8 am.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. To time them and fill in their words (your next level, your world and how much of it is lit), the App keeps the time you last opened it and the hour you usually play. There is no push-notification server and no notification token is sent anywhere.

Turn them off with Settings › Reminders in the game, or in Android’s notification settings.

Your file

Backup file

Settings › Backup file › Save writes a small file with your next level, booster counts, misses per level and how many pieces you have met. Android’s own file picker lets you choose where it goes, such as your phone’s storage or a cloud drive you already use; if you pick a cloud drive, that provider’s terms apply to the file.

Restore reads a file you pick and asks before replacing your progress. The App opens only files you choose, and we never receive a copy. Sky Spark Plus is not in the file; Restore purchases brings it back through Google Play.

On device only

Home-screen widgets

If you add a Sky Spark widget, it shows your next level or your current world, drawn on your phone from the progress above. It refreshes about twice a day and sends nothing anywhere.

03. What our server receives

Checking purchases, the optional account and cloud save run on a server operated by NRDDA Tech (GameHub). All traffic to it uses HTTPS. We do not sell or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Automatic

Anonymous player

When the App first connects, the server creates an anonymous player account for this install, with no name, email or phone number attached.

Data received: a random ID for this install, the App and Android version, your IP address (used to keep the service secure and prevent misuse), and the Google Play purchase token for Sky Spark Plus, so the server can confirm the purchase with Google and keep it with your account.

Optional

Save with email

You can save your game to your email address so it can come back on a new phone. We email you a one-time code to confirm it is yours; there is no password.

Cloud save (for the person who bought Sky Spark Plus): the App keeps a copy of your next level, coins, booster counts, misses per level, how many pieces you have met, and your music, sound, vibration and calm-mode settings. Ad counters, reminders and widgets stay on your phone.

04. What Google’s services collect

The App uses the following Google services. Each receives data directly from your device under Google’s own privacy policy, acting as a service provider to us.

Advertising

Google AdMob

The App shows a banner ad on the Home screen (never on the puzzle board). From level 20 it may show an interstitial ad after you clear a level: at most one every few levels, never right after another, and a limited number per day. You can choose to watch a rewarded video ad to get a booster (an extra match, an undo or a peek); these are always your choice.

Data collected by AdMob: the Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, language, mobile network, coarse location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and anti-fraud signals.

Purpose: showing ads, measuring them, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choice (see section 06) and your device’s ad settings.

Updates

Google Play In-App Updates

The App asks Google Play, on your phone, whether a newer version of the game is out. If one is, it may show a panel on Home (at most every few days) and one notification per new version, and the Update button hands over to Google Play. You can always choose Later.

Data processed: the check is handled by the Google Play Store app already on your phone, under Google’s privacy policy. It does not send your game progress anywhere.

05. Optional purchase

The game is fully playable without buying anything.

Optional

Purchases through Google Play

The App sells one thing: Sky Spark Plus, a one-time purchase that removes the banner and the interstitial ads and adds cloud save. Rewarded videos stay available if you want boosters from them.

Data processed: product ID, purchase token, order ID and purchase status, used to give you what you bought and to restore it after a reinstall or on a new phone (Settings › Restore purchases). The purchase token also goes to our server, which confirms it with Google and keeps it with your account.

Shared on the buyer’s phone: Sky Spark Plus bought on a phone also unlocks it for other Sky Spark accounts that sign in on that same phone, up to five (a family member, or your own second email). Our server records which accounts share the purchase; they see nothing of the buyer’s account. Cloud save stays with the account that bought it. A shared account keeps Plus on any phone it signs in on, until the purchase is refunded, which ends it for every account it was shared with.

Payment details: payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card number, bank details or billing address.

06. Ad consent

In places where the law requires your permission before ads can use your data, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. If you decline personalised ads, AdMob shows only non-personalised ads.

Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy options in the game.

Everywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some phones). Deleting it stops personalised ads in every app on your device.

09. Children

Sky Spark is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU). If you believe a child has used the App and you want their data removed, email us and we will help, including by contacting Google on your behalf.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: open the game and go to Settings › Privacy options (shown where consent applies).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Remove ads: buy Sky Spark Plus from the Shop or Settings.
  • Delete your account: signed in, open Settings › Account › Delete account. Your email and cloud save are deleted from our server after 14 days; sign in again within those 14 days to cancel. Not signed in, email us and we will delete the anonymous player for that install.
  • Reminders: turn them off with Settings › Reminders in the game, or in Android’s notification settings.
  • Backup files: they are yours; delete them wherever you saved them.
  • Local game data: Settings › Start over in the game, or Android Settings › Apps › Sky Spark › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the App.
  • Access or deletion requests: email us. We will send you what our server holds about you, delete it on request, tell you what the App stores on your phone, and work with Google on anything held by AdMob or Google Play. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. This applies under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and California’s CCPA/CPRA, and we extend it to everyone wherever they live.

11. Security and retention

  • In transit: all traffic to our server and to Google services uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Our server: your player account, purchases and (with Plus and email) cloud save are kept while you use the App, and deleted 14 days after you delete your account.
  • On device: game data is kept in the App’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox.
  • Ads and purchases: kept by Google (AdMob and Google Play) under its own retention policies.
  • Local data: kept until you clear it or uninstall the App.
  • Backup files: kept where you saved them until you delete them; uninstalling the App does not remove them.

No system is completely secure, but we use the protections above and collect as little as we can.

12. Changes to this policy

If the App starts collecting something new, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date at the top. The 9 November 2026 update added purchases shared with other accounts on the buyer’s phone. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the App’s update notes.
