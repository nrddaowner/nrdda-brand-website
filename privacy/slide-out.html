01. Overview

Slide Out (“the App”, “we”, “our”) is a casual puzzle game for Android, published by NRDDA Tech, Hyderabad, India. This policy applies only to this App.

Key takeaways

  • No account, ever. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos or location.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Levels, stars, hearts, hints, streaks, themes and settings are stored in the App’s private storage.
  • We run no server for this game and the App contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Anything that leaves your device goes to a Google service, never to a server of ours.
  • Game settings come from Firebase. The App downloads settings such as how often ads appear and how many hearts you get from Google’s Firebase Remote Config. This uses a random installation ID, not your identity.
  • The game is ad-supported. Ads come from Google AdMob. A one-time purchase, Slide Out Plus, removes interstitial and banner ads.
  • We never sell your personal information, to anyone.

02. What stays on your device

On device only

Game progress and settings

Stored: levels cleared, stars, hearts and their refill timer, hints, daily puzzle results and streak, themes and items you own or use, which new pieces you have already been shown, the themes Slide Out Plus has unlocked, and your sound, music, haptics, motion and reminder settings.

Also on device: simple counters the App uses to pace itself, such as how many times you have opened the game and how recently an ad was shown. These never leave your phone.

Removing it: clearing the App’s data or uninstalling deletes all of it. We have no copy.

On device only

Reminders and widgets

Daily puzzle and streak reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. There is no push-notification server and no notification token is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the game’s Settings or in Android’s notification settings.

When Google Play has a newer version of the App, it may post one local notification for that version, on its own “Updates” notification channel that you can switch off in Android’s settings.

Home-screen widgets, if you add them, read your progress from the App’s private storage on the same phone. They send nothing anywhere.

You choose

Backups and sharing

Back up progress writes your save to a file in a place you pick, such as your phone’s storage or a cloud drive app. Restore from backup reads a file you pick. The App only touches the one file you choose and does not need access to your other files.

When you tap Share, the App hands a short text about your result to the app you pick (for example, a messaging app). We do not receive it.

03. What Google’s services collect

The App uses the following Google services. Each receives data directly from your device under Google’s own privacy policy, acting as a service provider to us.

Advertising

Google AdMob

The App shows no ads during your first few sessions. After that it shows interstitial ads between some levels (at most one every few levels, and never right after another full-screen ad), a banner ad on menu screens (never on the puzzle board), a native ad (an advertiser's card, clearly marked “Ad”) on the streak screen, and rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch to refill your hearts or get a hint.

Data collected by AdMob: the Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, language, mobile network, coarse location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and anti-fraud signals.

Purpose: showing ads, measuring them, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choice (see section 05) and your device’s ad settings.

Game settings

Firebase Remote Config

The App downloads its game settings from Firebase Remote Config, a Google service. These settings decide how often ads appear, how many hearts you hold and how fast they refill, and when the App offers a hint, a skip or an update. They let us tune the game without shipping a new version. The game always works offline with the settings it already has.

Data collected by Firebase: a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier created for this install of the App, not linked to your name, account or Advertising ID), the App’s version, the device’s language, country and time zone, Android version, and the IP address the request comes from.

Purpose: delivering the right settings to this install. We do not use it for advertising or analytics, and Firebase Analytics is not included in the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes the installation ID from your phone; Google deletes the server copy under its Firebase retention terms.

Updates

Google Play In-App Updates

The App asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, and can then show Google Play’s own update screen. Downloading and installing the update are handled entirely by Google Play. Rarely, when an old version can no longer work properly, the App may ask you to update before you keep playing.

Ratings

Google Play In-App Review

After you have played for a while, the App may ask Google Play to show its rating prompt. The prompt, your rating and any review are handled entirely by Google Play. We see only what you post publicly on the store.

04. Optional features

The following only happen if you choose them. The game is fully playable without them.

Optional

Purchases through Google Play

The App sells one thing: Slide Out Plus, a one-time purchase that removes interstitial and banner ads, gives unlimited hearts, and unlocks every theme, including themes added later. Rewarded videos stay available if you want them.

Data processed: product ID, purchase token, order ID and purchase status, used to give you what you bought and to restore it after a reinstall or on a new phone.

Payment details: payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card number, bank details or billing address.

05. Ad consent

In places where the law requires your permission before ads can use your data, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. If you decline personalised ads, AdMob shows only non-personalised ads.

Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Ad choices in the game.

Everywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some phones). Deleting it stops personalised ads in every app on your device.

08. Children

Slide Out is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU). If you believe a child has used the App and you want their data removed, email us and we will help, including by contacting Google on your behalf.

09. Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: open the game and go to Settings › Ad choices (shown where consent applies).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Remove ads: buy Slide Out Plus from the Themes screen or Settings.
  • Local game data: Android Settings › Apps › Slide Out › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the App.
  • Access or deletion requests: email us. We hold no personal data about you ourselves, so we will tell you what the App stores on your phone and work with Google on anything held by AdMob, Firebase or Google Play. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. This applies under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and California’s CCPA/CPRA, and we extend it to everyone wherever they live.

10. Security and retention

  • In transit: all traffic to Google services uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • On device: game data is kept in the App’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox.
  • Backups: a backup file you create is kept wherever you saved it, until you delete it.
  • Ads, purchases and game settings: kept by Google (AdMob, Google Play and Firebase) under its own retention policies.
  • Local data: kept until you clear it or uninstall the App.

No system is completely secure, but we use the protections above and collect as little as we can.
