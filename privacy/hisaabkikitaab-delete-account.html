Delete your Hisaabo account

How to delete your account and data in Hisaabo: My Expense Manager (also shown as Irapot, formerly HisaabKiKitaab), published by NRDDA Tech. You can do it inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

01. Delete your account in the app

Steps

  • Open Hisaabo and sign in.
  • Tap Me in the bottom bar.
  • Tap your profile card at the top to open Your account.
  • Tap Delete account, then Delete to confirm.
  • Enter your password to prove it is you. Your account and its cloud data are deleted straight away.

To also remove the copy on your phone, uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings › Apps › Hisaabo › Storage › Clear data.

02. Delete your account by email

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, email nrdda.owner@gmail.com with the subject “Hisaabo account deletion”. Send it from the email address you signed up with, so we can confirm the account is yours.

We delete the account and its data within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm when it is done.

Email a deletion request

03. What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address, name and password record).
  • Your ledger in the cloud: every entry, category, budget, goal, recurring entry and setting saved under your account.
  • Your edit history and any data linked to your account ID on our servers.

04. What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records: Google Play keeps records of any purchases for tax and refund purposes. We cannot delete these; Google sets how long they are kept.
  • Analytics: de-identified usage statistics that can no longer be linked to you, kept for up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: kept for about 90 days, then deleted automatically.

For the full details, read the Hisaabo privacy policy.
