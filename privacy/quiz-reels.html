01. Overview

Quiz Reels (“the App”, “we”, “our”) is an exam-practice app for Android, published by NRDDA Tech, Hyderabad, India. This policy applies only to this App. Quiz Reels is an independent app and is not linked to the Staff Selection Commission or any government body.

Key takeaways

  • No account, no sign-in. We never ask for your email address, phone number, photos or location.
  • Your study progress stays on your phone, encrypted. Answers, streaks, saved cards and settings are sealed with a key held in Android’s hardware-backed Keystore.
  • We run no server for this app and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Anything that leaves your device goes to a Google service, never to a server of ours.
  • The app is ad-supported. Ads come from Google AdMob. A one-time purchase, Ad-free, removes the ads in the feed. No study feature is ever locked behind it.
  • We never sell your personal information, to anyone.

02. What stays on your device

On device only

Study progress and settings

Stored: the questions you answered and whether you got them right, how long you took, cards you saved, your revision schedule, study sets and sprint results, daily goal and streak (including streak freezes), topics you follow in the feed, and your language, theme, sound and reminder settings.

Question reports: if you report a question as wrong or unclear, the reason and any note you type are saved on your phone. They are not sent to us.

Also on device: simple counters the App uses to pace itself, such as the day you installed it and when it last offered Ad-free. These never leave your phone.

Encryption: the study database is encrypted with SQLCipher and your profile with AES-256-GCM, using a key that never leaves the Android Keystore. The App also opts out of Android’s automatic cloud backup, so these files are not copied off your phone.

Removing it: clearing the App’s data or uninstalling deletes all of it. We have no copy.

On device only

Daily reminder

The daily study reminder is scheduled locally on your phone at the time you pick. There is no push-notification server and no notification token is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the App or in Android’s notification settings.

You choose

Backups and sharing

Back up progress writes your progress to a .qrb file in a place you pick, or hands it to an app you choose, such as a cloud drive, mail or a messaging app. Restore reads a file you pick. The App only touches the one file you choose and does not need access to your other files. A backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. Your Ad-free purchase is never stored in it; Google Play restores that.

When you tap Share, the App creates an image of your result or a question card and hands it, with a link to the App, to the app you pick. We do not receive it.

03. What Google’s services collect

The App uses the following Google services. Each receives data directly from your device under Google’s own privacy policy, acting as a service provider to us.

Advertising

Google AdMob

The App shows two kinds of ads: a native ad (an advertiser’s card, clearly marked “Ad”) that takes one page every few reels in the feed, and rewarded video ads that play only when you tap to watch one, for example to save your streak. There are no ads that pop up between questions on their own.

Data collected by AdMob: the Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, language, mobile network, coarse location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and anti-fraud signals. On newer Android versions AdMob may also use Android’s Privacy Sandbox (ad topics and attribution), which you control in Android’s privacy settings.

Purpose: showing ads, measuring them, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choice (see section 05) and your device’s ad settings.

App settings

Firebase Remote Config

The App checks Firebase Remote Config about twice a day for a few settings, such as how often an ad card appears in the feed and when Ad-free is offered. This lets us tune ad frequency without shipping an update.

Data processed: a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier for this install of the App, not linked to you), the App version, and basic device information such as language and country, which Google needs to deliver the right settings.

Not collected: your answers, scores, progress or anything you type. Remote Config sends settings to the App; it does not receive your study data.

04. Optional features

The following only happen if you choose them. Every study feature works without them.

Optional

Purchases through Google Play

The App sells one thing: Ad-free, a one-time purchase that removes the ads in the feed. It does not unlock any questions or study features; those are free for everyone.

Data processed: product ID, purchase token, order ID and purchase status, used to give you what you bought and to restore it after a reinstall or on a new phone. The App checks this with Google Play on each launch, so a refunded purchase brings ads back.

Payment details: payments, including UPI, are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card number, UPI ID, bank details or billing address.

05. Ad consent

In places where the law requires your permission before ads can use your data, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. If you decline personalised ads, AdMob shows only non-personalised ads.

To change that choice later, clear the App’s data (Android Settings › Apps › Quiz Reels › Storage › Clear data) and the form will be shown again on the next launch. Back up your progress first if you want to keep it.

Everywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some phones). Deleting it stops personalised ads in every app on your device.

08. Children

Quiz Reels is made for adults preparing for competitive exams and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU, or 18 under India’s DPDP Act). If you believe a child has used the App and you want their data removed, email us and we will help, including by contacting Google on your behalf.

09. Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: clear the App’s data to see Google’s consent form again (shown where consent applies).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Remove ads: buy Ad-free from the crown on the Home screen.
  • Reminders: turn them off in the App or in Android’s notification settings.
  • Local study data: Android Settings › Apps › Quiz Reels › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the App.
  • Access or deletion requests: email us. We hold no personal data about you ourselves, so we will tell you what the App stores on your phone and work with Google on anything held by AdMob, Firebase or Google Play. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. This applies under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and California’s CCPA/CPRA, and we extend it to everyone wherever they live.

10. Security and retention

  • In transit: all traffic to Google services uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • On device: study data is encrypted (SQLCipher and AES-256-GCM, key in the Android Keystore) inside the App’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox.
  • Backups: a backup file you create is kept wherever you saved or sent it, until you delete it.
  • Ads, settings and purchases: kept by Google under its own retention policies.
  • Local data: kept until you clear it or uninstall the App.

No system is completely secure, but we use the protections above and collect as little as we can.
