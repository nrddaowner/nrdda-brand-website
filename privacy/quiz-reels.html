01. Overview

Quiz Reels (“the App”, “we”, “our”) is an exam-practice app for Android, published by NRDDA Tech, Hyderabad, India. This policy applies only to this App. Quiz Reels is an independent app and is not linked to the Staff Selection Commission or any government body.

Key takeaways

  • No sign-in needed. Every question works without one. Signing in with your email is optional and only puts you on the leaderboard and keeps your points across phones. We never ask for your phone number, photos or location.
  • Your study progress stays on your phone, encrypted. Answers, streaks, saved cards and settings are sealed with a key held in Android’s hardware-backed Keystore.
  • Points and the leaderboard run on our own server. It receives the answers it needs to award points, an anonymous player ID, and, only if you sign in, your email and the name you show on the board. The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • Cloud backup comes only with QuizReels Gold. Without Gold, your study progress never leaves your phone.
  • Reminders are worked out on your phone. Which reminder you get, and its words, come from your progress on the device. Only “new topic” messages come from Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging, and you can switch them off.
  • The app is ad-supported. Ads come from Google AdMob. A one-time purchase, QuizReels Gold, removes the ads and adds extras (automatic backup, characters, a crown on the leaderboard). No question or study feature is ever locked behind it.
  • We never sell your personal information, to anyone.

02. What stays on your device

On device only

Study progress and settings

Stored: the questions you answered and whether you got them right, how long you took, cards you saved, your revision schedule, study sets and sprint results, daily goal and streak (including streak freezes), topics you follow in the feed, and your language, theme, sound and reminder settings.

Question reports: if you report a question as wrong or unclear, the reason and any note you type are saved on your phone. They are not sent to us.

Also on device: simple counters the App uses to pace itself, such as the day you installed it and when it last offered QuizReels Gold. These never leave your phone.

Encryption: the study database is encrypted with SQLCipher and your profile with AES-256-GCM, using a key that never leaves the Android Keystore. The App also opts out of Android’s automatic cloud backup, so these files are not copied off your phone.

Removing it: clearing the App’s data or uninstalling deletes all of it from your phone. What our server holds (section 03) is deleted separately, as described there.

On device only

Study reminders

Reminders are off until you turn them on, and Android asks for your permission first. Once on, they are scheduled locally on your phone: one a day at the time you pick, plus at most one evening reminder when a streak of three days or more would break that night.

Which reminder you get (for example questions due for revision, your streak, a weekly summary or a question of the day) and the numbers in it are worked out on your phone from your study progress. None of this is sent anywhere. The App also notes the last day it was opened, so it can stop reminding you after 30 days away.

Turn reminders off in the App (Settings › Notifications) or in Android’s notification settings.

You choose

Backups and sharing

Back up progress writes your progress to a .qrb file in a place you pick, or hands it to an app you choose, such as a cloud drive, mail or a messaging app. Restore reads a file you pick. The App only touches the one file you choose and does not need access to your other files. A backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. Your QuizReels Gold purchase is never stored in it; Google Play restores that.

When you tap Share, the App creates an image of your result or a question card and hands it, with a link to the App, to the app you pick. We do not receive it.

03. What our server receives

Points, the weekly leaderboard, optional sign-in and QuizReels Gold’s cloud backup run on a server operated by NRDDA Tech and hosted in India. All traffic to it uses HTTPS. We do not sell or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Automatic

Anonymous player and points

When the App first connects, the server creates an anonymous player account for this install. It has no name, email or phone number attached.

Data received: a random install ID made by the App, the App and Android version, and your IP address (kept with your device record to stop abuse such as fake accounts and point farming). For points, the App sends which question you answered, the option you picked and how long you took; the server checks the answer against its own copy of the questions and adds up your points, streak and weekly total.

Not received: your saved cards, reports, revision schedule, reminders or anything you type, unless you use cloud backup below.

Optional

Sign in with email

You can sign in with your email address to appear on the leaderboard and keep your points on a new phone. We email you a one-time code to confirm it is yours; there is no password.

Data received: your email address and the name you choose for the leaderboard.

Shown to other people: only on the leaderboard, your chosen name, your points and rank, your character or letter, and a gold crown if you hold QuizReels Gold. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

QuizReels Gold

Cloud backup and characters

With QuizReels Gold the App keeps a copy of your study progress on our server, so it comes back on a new phone: the cards you answered, saved or liked and how well you know each, your daily totals and streak days, your progress in each study set, your daily goal and your language choices. It syncs when you open the App and while you study. The answer history, question reports and sprint results stay on your phone. Older copies are removed after 7 days.

The character or letter you pick is stored with your account so others see it on the leaderboard.

Automatic

Question packs

New questions are downloaded as files from our server. These downloads carry no information about you beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address).

04. What Google’s services collect

The App uses the following Google services. Each receives data directly from your device under Google’s own privacy policy, acting as a service provider to us.

Advertising

Google AdMob

The App shows two kinds of ads: a native ad (an advertiser’s card, clearly marked “Ad”) that takes one page every few reels in the feed, and rewarded video ads that play only when you tap to watch one, for example to save your streak. There are no ads that pop up between questions on their own.

Data collected by AdMob: the Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, language, mobile network, coarse location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and anti-fraud signals. On newer Android versions AdMob may also use Android’s Privacy Sandbox (ad topics and attribution), which you control in Android’s privacy settings.

Purpose: showing ads, measuring them, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choice (see section 06) and your device’s ad settings.

App settings

Firebase Remote Config

The App checks Firebase Remote Config about twice a day for a few settings, such as how often an ad card appears in the feed, when QuizReels Gold is offered, whether points and new question packs are switched on, and whether a newer version of the App is out. This lets us tune these without shipping an update.

Data processed: a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier for this install of the App, not linked to you), the App version, and basic device information such as language and country, which Google needs to deliver the right settings.

Not collected: your answers, scores, progress or anything you type. Remote Config sends settings to the App; it does not receive your study data.

Messages

Firebase Cloud Messaging

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send occasional messages to everyone who uses the App, such as “a new topic has been added”. Messages go to a topic for your App language (Hindi or English), not to you personally, and we do not keep a list of devices.

Data processed: a Firebase installation ID and a messaging token (random identifiers for this install of the App, not linked to you), the App version and basic device information, which Google needs to deliver the message.

Turning it off: switch off New topics in Settings › Notifications, and the App leaves the topic. Android’s notification settings also stop them.

05. Optional purchase

This only happens if you choose it. Every question and study feature works without it.

Optional

Purchases through Google Play

The App sells one thing: QuizReels Gold, a one-time purchase that removes the ads and adds automatic cloud backup, characters, a crown on the leaderboard and saving your streak without an ad. It does not unlock any questions; those are free for everyone.

Data processed: product ID, purchase token, order ID and purchase status, used to give you what you bought and to restore it after a reinstall or on a new phone. The App checks this with Google Play on each launch, and also sends the purchase token to our server, which confirms it directly with Google Play before turning on the Gold features. A refunded purchase turns them off.

Payment details: payments, including UPI, are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card number, UPI ID, bank details or billing address.

06. Ad consent

In places where the law requires your permission before ads can use your data, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. If you decline personalised ads, AdMob shows only non-personalised ads.

To change that choice later, clear the App’s data (Android Settings › Apps › Quiz Reels › Storage › Clear data) and the form will be shown again on the next launch. Back up your progress first if you want to keep it.

Everywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some phones). Deleting it stops personalised ads in every app on your device.

09. Children

Quiz Reels is made for adults preparing for competitive exams and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU, or 18 under India’s DPDP Act). If you believe a child has used the App and you want their data removed, email us and we will help, including by contacting Google on your behalf.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: clear the App’s data to see Google’s consent form again (shown where consent applies).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Remove ads: buy QuizReels Gold from the crown on the Home screen.
  • Leaderboard name: change it any time in Settings by tapping your name.
  • Delete your account: signed in, go to Settings › Delete account. Your points, streak, leaderboard name, character and cloud backup are deleted from our server after 14 days; sign in again within those 14 days to cancel. Not signed in, email us and we will delete the anonymous player for that install.
  • Reminders and new-topic messages: turn each off in the App (Settings › Notifications) or all of them in Android’s notification settings.
  • Local study data: Android Settings › Apps › Quiz Reels › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the App.
  • Access or deletion requests: email us. We will send you what our server holds about you, delete it on request, tell you what the App stores on your phone, and work with Google on anything held by AdMob, Firebase or Google Play. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. This applies under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and California’s CCPA/CPRA, and we extend it to everyone wherever they live.

11. Security and retention

  • In transit: all traffic to our server and to Google services uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Our server: your player account, points and (with Gold) cloud backup are kept while you use the App, and deleted 14 days after you delete your account. Older cloud-backup copies are removed after 7 days.
  • On device: study data is encrypted (SQLCipher and AES-256-GCM, key in the Android Keystore) inside the App’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox.
  • Backups: a backup file you create is kept wherever you saved or sent it, until you delete it.
  • Ads, settings and purchases: kept by Google under its own retention policies.
  • Local data: kept until you clear it or uninstall the App.

No system is completely secure, but we use the protections above and collect as little as we can.
