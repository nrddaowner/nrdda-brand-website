01. Overview

Ira’s Riddles (“the App”, “we”, “our”) is a logic riddle game for Android, with 100 levels in five packs and a Daily Riddle, published by NRDDA Tech, Hyderabad, India. Every level and riddle ships inside the App and plays offline. This policy applies only to this App.

Key takeaways

  • No sign-up needed. The game works without an account. Saving your game with your email is optional, and we never ask for your phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your progress lives on your phone. If you save your game with your email, a copy is also kept on our server so it can come back on a new phone, and your scores can appear on the leaderboards.
  • A small server of ours (GameHub, run by NRDDA Tech) checks purchases and keeps the optional account, cloud save, leaderboards and badges. The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or Firebase SDK.
  • The daily reminder is set on your phone. It is off until you turn it on. There is no push server.
  • The game is ad-supported. Ads come from Google AdMob. A one-time purchase, Ira’s Riddles Plus, removes every ad and adds hints.
  • We never sell your personal information, to anyone.

02. What stays on your device

On device only

Game progress and settings

Stored: the stars you have won on each level, the levels you skipped, how many hints and other helpers you hold, the days you solved the Daily Riddle (your streak), the Sunday gifts and packs you have finished, which first-time guides you have seen, the badges you have won, whether you own Ira’s Riddles Plus, and your sound, music, vibration and reminder settings.

Also on device: how many reward videos you have watched today (at most five a day) and a few small markers the App uses for itself, such as when your game was last backed up. The counters that pace ads between levels are kept only in memory and are gone when the App closes.

Removing it: clearing the App’s data or uninstalling deletes what is on your phone.

Your Google account

Android backup

If Android’s own backup is on for your phone, Android can copy the App’s progress file (stars, helpers, streak and settings) to your Google account’s backup, or move it to a new phone when you set one up, so a reinstall can pick up where you left off. This is run by Android and Google under Google’s privacy policy; we never receive it. The App limits the backup to that one file: your sign-in and everything else stays on the phone.

You can switch it off in Android Settings › Google › Backup (the name varies by phone).

On device only

Daily Riddle reminder

The game can remind you, once a day at about 6 PM, that a new Daily Riddle is ready. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, and on Android 13 and newer Android asks for your permission first.

The reminder is scheduled locally on your phone and its words are always the same. There is no push-notification server and no notification token is sent anywhere.

Turn it off with Settings › Daily Riddle reminder in the game, or in Android’s notification settings.

03. What our server receives

Checking purchases, the optional account, cloud save, leaderboards and badges run on a server operated by NRDDA Tech (GameHub). All traffic to it uses HTTPS. We do not sell or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Automatic

Anonymous player

When the App first connects, the server creates an anonymous player account for this install, with no name, email or phone number attached.

Data received: a random ID made for this install (not a hardware or advertising ID), the App version and platform, your IP address (used to keep the service secure and prevent misuse), and the Google Play purchase token for Ira’s Riddles Plus if you buy it, so the server can confirm the purchase with Google and keep it with your account.

Optional

Save your game with email

You can save your game to your email address so it can come back on a new phone. We email you a 6-digit code to confirm it is yours; there is no password. The account is an NRDDA Games account: the same email can be used in other NRDDA games. It is signed in on one phone at a time.

Cloud save (free for anyone who saves with email): the App keeps a copy of your stars per level, skipped levels, hint and helper counts, Daily Riddle days, Sunday gifts and finished packs, the guides you have seen, your Plus hint days, and your sound, music and vibration settings. The reward-video counter, the reminder and the ad counters stay on your phone.

Leaderboards and badges (only once you save with email): your board name and your scores on four boards — Today’s Riddle (your solving time plus a penalty for wrong taps), Total Stars, This Week and Best Streak — and your progress towards the game’s badges. Other players see your board name, your scores and your place, and a crown if you own Plus. Your board name is “Player” with a number until you choose one in Settings › Change name; please do not use your real full name. Your email is never shown to other players.

04. What Google’s services collect

The App uses the following Google services. Each receives data directly from your device under Google’s own privacy policy, acting as a service provider to us.

Advertising

Google AdMob

The App shows a banner ad at the foot of the Home, Levels, Leaderboards, Badges, Shop and Settings screens (never while you are solving a level or the Daily Riddle). Once you have solved 10 levels it may show an interstitial ad after you clear a level: at most one every third level, at least two minutes apart, and never after the Daily Riddle. You can choose to watch a rewarded video ad to get a hint, up to five a day; these are always your choice.

Data collected by AdMob: the Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, language, mobile network, coarse location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and anti-fraud signals.

Purpose: showing ads, measuring them, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choice (see section 06) and your device’s ad settings.

Updates

Google Play In-App Updates

The App asks Google Play, on your phone, whether a newer version of the game is out. If one is, it shows a message on Home and the Update button hands over to Google Play. You can usually choose Later; an urgent update (for example a serious fix) may have to be taken before you keep playing.

Data processed: the check is handled by the Google Play Store app already on your phone, under Google’s privacy policy. It does not send your game progress anywhere.

05. Optional purchase

The game is fully playable without buying anything. No level, pack or cloud save is behind a purchase.

Early access: Plus is not on sale yet in the early-access build. What follows is how it works once it is.

Optional

Purchases through Google Play

The App sells one thing: Ira’s Riddles Plus, a one-time purchase that removes every ad (banners, ads between levels and reward videos), gives 30 hints at once and 3 more every day, and adds a crown by your name on the leaderboards. With Plus, the hints a video would give come without the video.

Data processed: product ID, purchase token, order ID and purchase status, used to give you what you bought and to restore it after a reinstall or on a new phone (Settings › Restore purchase). The purchase token also goes to our server, which confirms it with Google and keeps it with your account, so Plus can follow your saved game to a new phone.

Payment details: payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card number, bank details or billing address.

06. Ad consent

Early access: the early-access build shows only Google’s test ads, and no real ad is requested. Before real ads are switched on, the App will show Google’s consent form in places where the law requires your permission before ads can use your data, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and a Settings › Privacy options entry will let you change your choice. If you decline personalised ads, AdMob will show only non-personalised ads. We will update this page when that happens.

Everywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some phones). Deleting it stops personalised ads in every app on your device.

09. Children

Ira’s Riddles is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU). If you believe a child has used the App and you want their data removed, email us and we will help, including by contacting Google on your behalf.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: once real ads are switched on, open the game and go to Settings › Privacy options (shown where consent applies). See section 06.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Remove ads: buy Ira’s Riddles Plus from the Shop or Settings.
  • Board name: change it with Settings › Account › Change name.
  • Delete your account: signed in, open Settings › Account › Delete account. Your email, cloud save, board name, scores and badges are deleted from our server after 14 days, in every NRDDA game that uses the same account; sign in again within those 14 days to cancel. The progress on your phone stays. Not signed in, email us and we will delete the anonymous player for that install.
  • Reminder: turn it off with Settings › Daily Riddle reminder in the game, or in Android’s notification settings.
  • Android backup: switch it off, or delete the backup, in Android Settings › Google › Backup.
  • Local game data: Android Settings › Apps › Ira’s Riddles › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the App.
  • Access or deletion requests: email us. We will send you what our server holds about you, delete it on request, tell you what the App stores on your phone, and work with Google on anything held by AdMob or Google Play. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. This applies under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and California’s CCPA/CPRA, and we extend it to everyone wherever they live.

11. Security and retention

  • In transit: all traffic to our server and to Google services uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Our server: your player account, purchases and (with email) cloud save, board name, scores and badges are kept while you use the App, and deleted 14 days after you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: a record of each Google Play purchase is kept after your account is deleted, no longer linked to you, as a financial record and to prevent the same receipt being used twice.
  • On device: game data is kept in the App’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox.
  • Ads and purchases: kept by Google (AdMob and Google Play) under its own retention policies.
  • Local data: kept until you clear it or uninstall the App.
  • Android backup: kept in your Google account under Google’s backup settings until you delete it.

No system is completely secure, but we use the protections above and collect as little as we can.
