What's inside

One question on the screen, the reason right after

Swipe like reels

Swipe up for the next question, tap your answer and see right away whether you got it.

An explanation every time

Every question comes with a short explanation, so you learn the reason and not just the answer.

Concept cards first

Short cards with the idea come before each set, and wrong answers come back a few reels later.

Playlists, easy to hard

Go through a subject topic by topic, with three levels in every topic.

हिंदी or English

Use the app in either language and switch any time. Dark mode for late sessions.

Offline, no sign-in

Questions are built in. No account, progress encrypted on your phone, with a backup file you can restore on a new one.
