What's inside

Fireworks puzzles over India’s festival skies

Easy to start

Three simple rules, no timers, and the shells never move. Every board has exactly one winning set of launchers.

20 festival worlds

Diwali in Jaipur, the monsoon in Mumbai, Durga Puja in Kolkata, Deepotsav in Ayodhya and more, each with its own sky.

20 firework pieces

Rain clouds that put out fire, star shells, chakris, anaars, ladis and more, each with its own rule.

500 hand-checked levels

Every level is solved by computer to make sure it has one answer and needs every matchstick you get.

A Daily Show

The same board for everyone each day: fewest matchsticks wins. Keep your streak and climb the leaders.

Peek and undo

Stuck? Peek shows a launcher’s whole chain before you fire, and Undo takes back your last matchstick.

Sky Spark Plus

One payment, yours forever

Sky Spark is free with ads. Plus is a one-time purchase that restores with your Google Play account.

  • No banner ads
  • No ads between levels
  • Cloud save for your progress
  • Every level and world stays free for everyone
