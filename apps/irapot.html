Irapot: Expenses & Split Bills

Track daily expenses, set budgets, never miss a bill, and split costs with friends.

You write every entry yourself. Irapot never connects to your bank, never reads your SMS and never asks for your card or UPI details.

Privacy policy
What's inside

A money diary for you, and Split for everyone else

Daily expenses in seconds

Amount, category, account and note on one screen. Cash, bank, card and wallet accounts, each with its own balance.

Budget planner

A budget for the month and for each category, a warning before you cross one, and an early look at this month’s pace.

See where it goes

Spending by category compared with last month, your biggest category, and PDF or Excel export.

Never miss a bill

Bills with due dates and a reminder before each one, plus a daily nudge to write the day’s spends.

Split bills with friends

Groups for a trip, a flat or the house. Friends join with a link, QR or code; split equally, by exact amounts, shares or percent.

Save more

Savings goals, a weekly challenge and no-spend days, with a streak to keep.

Private and safe

Your diary is encrypted on your phone, with a fingerprint or face lock. Sign in with your email and a one-time code.

Hindi and English

The whole app in Hindi or English, light or dark. Works without internet and catches up when you are back online.

Irapot Plus

Free, or Plus once

Irapot is free with ads; each new entry uses a few coins you get when you join and can earn every day. Plus is a one-time payment, not a subscription.

  • No coins and no ads
  • Repeating entries for rent, fees and fixed costs
  • Read bills and UPI screenshots to fill in an entry
  • Receipt photos, backed up to your account
  • Year view, compare months and a repeat-payment finder
  • Comments and reactions in Split

Need help?

Your data

Email sign-in with a one-time code. Your money diary is encrypted on your phone and kept on our own server so it comes back on a new phone; Split groups are shared only with their members. Ads by Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.
